Your PocketOBI on a big screen, with a memory.

Read a Makita battery on your computer, keep a file on every pack you have ever tested, and hand your customer a health card instead of a shrug.

Download Try it without a device

Free for personal use · Windows, macOS, Linux · English, Français, Deutsch, Español · nothing leaves your computer

Every reading, readable

  • Five cell bars, the spread between them, pack voltage, capacity and age.
  • A health estimate, the cycle count, over-discharge and overload counters, both temperatures.
  • One verdict, the same word the device shows: the decode and the verdict are ported from the PocketOBI firmware, so the two never disagree.

One click saves the reading into the pack's file.

A file on every pack

Each battery is tracked by its serial number. Read it today, read it again in three months: PackScope puts the two side by side.

  • Who and where. An alias, an owner (yours or a customer's), a status from "to diagnose" to "repaired" or "parts", and your own tags.
  • Verdict over time, one coloured mark per reading, so a pack that is sliding shows it.
  • Trends of pack voltage, cell spread, temperature spread and cycles, and a side-by-side compare of any two readings.
  • Repair sessions, with what was tried and whether the unlock held after a charge.

A health card for your customer

One button turns a pack's file into a clean one-page report: the verdict in words, the cells, the counters, the verdict history and every repair with its outcome.

It opens in your browser; print it, or save it as a PDF. Useful when a pack goes back to its owner, or when you sell one and want to show what you checked.

A diagnostic aid, not a warranty: the card says so.

The repair wizard, step by step

Read, Classify, Unlock, Confirm. PackScope reads the pack and tells a false lockout from a real fault before anything is written.

Classify

Charger lock, latched fault, thermistor and cells, each checked and shown, then a plain-language observation, likely cause and what to check.

Unlock

Through the PocketOBI, the same write the device makes. When it finds a hardware fault it says so, and tells you to fix that first.

Confirm

Re-read, log the session in the pack's file. Charge it afterwards: a pack with a real fault locks again, and its file shows that too.

In your language

English, Français, Deutsch, Español. Pick one in Settings and the whole app follows, verdicts and repair hints included, with the same words as the device.

The PocketOBI device itself also speaks Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese and Chinese: see the Asia firmware. Want PackScope in your language, or a better wording? Help translate.

Your data stays yours

  • Local. Every reading goes into one file on your computer. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.
  • CSV export with Makita-compatible columns, or the full set with PocketOBI's extra fields, for your own spreadsheet.
  • A JSON report you share only if you choose to.

Try it before you build

No PocketOBI yet? On the Connect page, pick a demo battery and use the demo bridge: a healthy pack, a false lockout, a dead thermistor. On the Batteries page, load demo data to see a few months of history.

With a device: put PocketOBI in PC bridge mode and plug it in over USB. If PackScope can't see it, the bridge troubleshooting steps cover it.
